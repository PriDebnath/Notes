# Notes

**A calm, intelligent space to capture thoughts — and explore them with AI 🤖.**

Notes turns your ideas, notes, and short writings into a personal library you enjoy browsing — not just storing.

## Great for

* Readers saving meaningful notes
* Writers collecting ideas and snippets
* Thinkers and journalers who enjoy short-form notes

  
## What you get

* **Card-based notes** that feel inviting and easy to read
* **Custom backgrounds & themes** to match your mood
* **Clean, distraction-free writing** with focus on your words
* **Simple organization** using tags, filters, and sorting
* **Fast, local-first performance with intelligent enhancements** that stays smooth as you grow
* **Context-aware responses** — AI understands the specific note you're viewing
* **Authentication and authorisation** to share your idea with the world.
*

## 🧠 Engineering & Technology

### ⚙️ Frontend
* Built with **React, Typescript, ShadCN, Tiptap**
* Modern **feature-driven architecture**
* Focus on **performance, accessibility, and responsive UI**
* Structured for **scalability and maintainability**

### 🛠️ Backend
* Built with **Express, MongoDB**
* Clean **API design and modular services**

### 🧪 Testing

* **Integration testing** for module interaction
* **End-to-End testing** using **Cypress** and **Playwright**
* Covers key user flows like creating, editing, and organizing notes

### 🤖 AI Integration
* Context-aware AI responses based on active note content
